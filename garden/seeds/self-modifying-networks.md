# Self-Modifying Neural Networks

**What it is**: A neural network that reshapes its own structure during training — grows neurons when needed, prunes when not, rewires connections. Meta-learner decides what to change. Rollback if performance drops.

**Why exotic**: Normal NNs learn weights. This one learns *architecture*. It's a network that builds itself while training.

**Link**: https://github.com/woodydrn/self-modifying-network

**Unflinching truth**: The idea is profound but the implementation is a prototype. Self-modification loops are fragile — one bad mutation cascade and the whole thing collapses. The rollback mechanism is essential but not proven at scale.
