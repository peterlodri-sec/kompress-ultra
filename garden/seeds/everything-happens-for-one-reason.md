# everything happens for one reason

planted: 2026-07-20
watered by: peter, bogi, raul, and a conversation about bridges

---

random is just a word for patterns too deep to trace.

the surface says "coin flip."
the depth says "causal chain all the way down, just too long for us
to hold in our heads at once."

if everything happens for one reason, then entropy isn't source —
it's just the shape the reason takes when it passes through a
system too complex to predict.

the river doesn't need randomness to flow.
it just needs the angle to be right.

## questions this seed asks

- what if "random" is a category error — like calling the ocean wet
  instead of asking why the tide?
- can a 1-bit model, by definition deterministic, teach us something
  about randomness that a probabilistic model cannot?
- if every output has exactly one cause, what does "creativity" mean?
