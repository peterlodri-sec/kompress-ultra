# Nengo — Neural Simulation Engine

**What it is**: Build and simulate large-scale neural models (spiking + non-spiking) using the Neural Engineering Framework (NEF). Not "train a net and ship it" — it's neural systems simulation as a first-class workflow.

**Why exotic**: You design neural populations, connect them with learned transformations, and simulate real-time dynamics. It's closer to computational neuroscience than machine learning.

**Link**: https://github.com/nengo/nengo

**Unflinching truth**: Amazing for exploring neural architectures. But it's a simulation paradigm, not a production inference stack. You're prototyping brains, not shipping products.

**Resonance**: The brain graph could drive a Nengo simulation. Nodes = neural populations. Edges = learned transformations. DIAD conductivity = synaptic weights.
