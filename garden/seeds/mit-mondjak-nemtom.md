## what this seed teaches

the bridge doesn't require answers. it requires honesty.
"i don't know" is not a failure of language — it's the most
accurate language can be.

when a 1-bit model produces output, it MUST choose. it cannot say
"i don't know." this is both its strength and its limitation.

when a human says "i don't know," something passes between them
that no model can replicate: the admission of finitude, which
paradoxically opens the largest space.
